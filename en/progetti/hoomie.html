Work/UI/UX

Hoomie

Finding a home and the right people to share it with, for 95% less than an agency would cost. The app for students and newcomers, now on the app stores and the web.

Client
Hoomie
Year
2024 — 2026
Role
Co-founder, CDO & CMO
What I did
App UI/UX, design system

Goal

Hoomie is a two-sided service: it simplifies the search for a home and matches the right people so they can find a place together. Compared with a typical agency fee (€651), it cuts the cost of searching by 95%.

Two kinds of users

Landlords. They list whole flats or single rooms and look for tenants, both groups and individuals.

Newcomers. They see listings sorted by compatibility and get suggestions of groups of people to move in with.

System map

For newcomers, the complexity lies in interacting with and exploring the options inside the app.

For landlords, the flow is designed to be as simple as possible, shifting the focus from "I have to find" to "I get found".

The main feature

People come before places.

Crew

What to communicate. The biggest challenge was making a simple idea easy to grasp: there are people you could live with really well, in certain homes.

The choice. People come before places. What you have in common stands out at a glance, and you can explore it further in a dedicated section.

The homes carousel. Showing the possible homes straight away lets you check the second part of the choice. The goal was to show both key elements of the decision on a single screen.

The profiles

Archetypes make comparable a kind of information that usually stays vague: not just "I'm tidy", but "you get along with people who keep things tidy".

The risk is reducing people to six boxes. By focusing on the positive side of each archetype, though, deciding gets faster while you explore.

For landlords

Reducing reliance on estate agents takes tools that show a property at its best. We focused on video tours, collected in a dedicated vertical feed, and on a quick, direct summary of the people who apply.

In development with external partners: in-app contracts, with choosing the right contract, entering the details and a full review before signing.

Design system

A single component system for both sides of the product: listing cards, profiles, groups, filters and onboarding questions.
