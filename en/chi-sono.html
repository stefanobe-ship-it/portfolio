In short

Then the move from branding to UI/UX: what pushed you and what you brought along. It's what sets you apart from people who come from only one of the two worlds.

How I use AI

To me, AI is an assistant. I'm the project manager.

I'm still working out where the line is, but for now this is how I see it: I hand it tasks, check the work and decide what to keep.

In Figma I use it for the repetitive parts: generating components and variants, switching a system from light to dark, retouching images without opening Photoshop.

The biggest effect, though, is on the process: getting to an interactive mock-up is much faster today, refining it is not.

This site is an example: I built it with Claude Code. The first version came together quickly, then came the rounds of refinement: rebuilding the slide text in HTML so it could be translated, removing effects that didn't work, fixing the covers.
