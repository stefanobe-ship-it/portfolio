In short

I graduated first from the Politecnico di Torino and then from the Politecnico di Milano. From the two I picked up some of their opposites and contradictions: Turin's rigour and technical focus, Milan's commercial sense and eye for aesthetics. I try to combine them as best I can.

Then the move from branding to UI/UX: what pushed you and what you brought along. It's what sets you apart from people who come from only one of the two worlds.
